Core Doctrine

D—01

Destructible Systems

Every turret, engine, radar and torpedo tube carries its own health. Shoot the engine and they crawl. Shoot the radar and they go blind. Shoot the rudder and they can't turn. Every hit rewrites the battle.

D—02

CS2 On Water

Harbor Siege: attack or defend a fortified harbor. One round, no respawn, every decision final. Three ships must hold the breach zone for ten straight seconds. Defenders stop them — or die trying.

D—03

Zero Pay-To-Win

Ships and stats are earned through play alone. Premium currency buys cosmetics and nothing else. No energy gates, no loot-box ships. The best player wins — not the biggest spender.

Battle Modes

// EVERY MODE CHANGES WHAT WINS
M—01

Harbor Siege

One round. No respawn. Attack the harbor or defend it. Three ships hold the breach zone for ten seconds — defenders survive or die. Every decision is final.

M—02

Fleet Annihilation

Sink all seven. Classic destruction. No objectives to game, nowhere to hide. The last fleet standing wins — and the score tracks who carried.

M—03

Control Points

Three strategic zones on the map. Hold more zones than your enemy to bleed their tickets dry. Momentum is everything — lose one point and the tide turns fast.
