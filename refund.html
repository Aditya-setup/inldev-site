Last updated: 21 November 2026.

Cancellation

You can cancel your EZStream Pro subscription at any time from the app (Profile → Cancel subscription) or by emailing malhotraaditya342@gmail.com. Auto-renewal stops immediately and you keep Pro until the end of the current billing period. No further charges will be made.

Refunds

  • 7-day refund: if you are not happy with your first Pro payment, email us within 7 days of payment and we will refund it in full.
  • Failed or duplicate payments: if you were charged twice or charged but Pro did not unlock, email us with your payment ID and we will refund the extra amount.
  • Renewals are not refundable once the new period has started, except for the cases above.
  • Approved refunds are sent back to the original payment method through Razorpay within 5–7 working days.

Digital product, no shipping

EZStream Pro is a digital service. Nothing is shipped; Pro unlocks in the app right after payment, on the Google account you signed in with.
