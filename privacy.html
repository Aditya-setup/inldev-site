Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026. This policy covers apps published by INL Dev Studio ("we", "us"), including SnapSolve, and the website inldev.in.

What we collect

  • Account info: if you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile photo through Firebase Authentication. You can also use the app as a guest without giving these.
  • What you send to the AI: questions you type and photos you scan (for example a textbook page) are sent to Google's Gemini model through Firebase to generate an answer.
  • Study history: your past questions, chats, notes and bookmarks are saved so you can see them later.
  • Purchases: if you buy Premium, Google Play handles payment. We only receive confirmation of your subscription, never your card details.
  • Ads: the free version may show ads from an ad network, which can use your device's advertising ID to show relevant ads. You can reset or limit this in your Android settings.
  • App diagnostics: basic crash and performance data to help fix bugs.

How we use it

To run the app, answer your questions, save your progress, manage your subscription and daily free answers, and improve the app. We do not sell your personal data.

Who we share it with

Only the services that make the app work: Google Firebase (sign-in, storage, AI), Google Play (payments) and our ad network partner. Each follows its own privacy policy.

Camera

The camera is used only when you choose to scan a question. Photos are not stored on our servers beyond what is needed to answer you, unless you save them to your history.

AI answers

Answers are generated by AI and can be wrong. Please check important information. You can report an inaccurate answer from inside the app.

Children

SnapSolve is designed for students. If a child under 13 uses it, we recommend a parent or guardian sets it up. We do not knowingly collect more data from children than the app needs to work, and guest mode needs no personal details.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while your account is active. To delete your account and all your data, use Settings → Delete account in the app, or email us at malhotraaditya342@gmail.com and we will delete it within 30 days.

EZStream (Windows)

  • Sign-in with Google gives us your name, email and photo (Firebase Authentication) to manage your Pro plan.
  • Stream keys and settings are stored only on your PC. Stream keys are masked in logs.
  • If you send Feedback, the app attaches your app log and system info (no stream keys) so we can fix bugs.
  • Pro payments are handled by Razorpay; we receive only payment status, never card or UPI details.
  • INL Cam sends your phone camera and mic only to your own PC over your local network; nothing passes through our servers.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and stored with Google Firebase. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Changes

If we update this policy, we will change the date at the top of this page.
