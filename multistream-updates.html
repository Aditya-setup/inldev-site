Every version, new feature and bug fix (57 releases). The app updates itself, or download the latest installer.

MAJORv1.8.02026.11.25
  • Go live window: title, description, tags with suggestions from popular videos, YouTube thumbnail, stream delay, and visibility (Public, Unlisted, Private, or Schedule for later).
  • Intro and outro can play in a corner so your game stays visible, or full screen. Skip them any time with the button or Ctrl+Shift+S.

  • Vertical 9:16 live for YouTube Shorts and mobile viewers (Performance mode).
  • Phone Camera: use your Android phone as a wireless camera and mic, now with lower delay.
  • Clearer voice: Native audio is the default; app volume sliders change only what viewers hear.
  • New app icon, invite-a-friend rewards, window no longer goes behind the taskbar, and many fixes.
